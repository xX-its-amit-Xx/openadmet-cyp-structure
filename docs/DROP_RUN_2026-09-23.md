### 11.1 What the organisers' scorer actually does — decisive

The Space (`huggingface.co/spaces/openadmet/cyp-challenge`) only gates on file count; the
scoring runs in a backend whose source is public: **`github.com/OpenADMET/blind-challenge-template`**
(`docs/structure-scoring.md`, `backend/evaluate_predictions.py::score_single_structure`,
read 2026-09-24). It is **OpenStructure**, not a bespoke scorer:

```python
model = PDBPrep(model_path, fault_tolerant=True); ref = PDBPrep(ref_path, ...)
model_lig = model.Select("rname=LIG and ele!=H")      # --heme keep is safe: heme is not LIG
SCRMSDScorer(model=..., target=..., substructure_match=True, coverage_delta=0.1)   # BiSyRMSD, LDDT-LP
LDDTPLIScorer(model=..., target=..., substructure_match=True, coverage_delta=0.1)  # LDDT-PLI
```

Neither constructor passes `resnum_alignments` or `seqres`. In OST
(`ligand_scoring_base.LigandScorerBase.__init__`) the default is **`resnum_alignments=False`**
and `seqres=None`, so `ChainMapper.seqres_set` is False and `_Align` takes the
`NWAlign` branch — **Needleman–Wunsch, BLOSUM62, gap −11/−1**. LDDT-PLI then builds its
binding site through `scorer._ProcessModel(mdl_bs, lddt_chain_mapping, residue_mapping=lddt_alns)`,
i.e. through those **alignments**, never through residue numbers.

> **The organisers pair protein residues by sequence alignment. Residue numbering is
> irrelevant to their LDDT-PLI.** FINDING 021 / T1 is a hazard of *our* scorer
> (`cypstruct.pose.lddt_pli` pairs by `(resnum, atom_name)`), not of theirs.

There is no reference or example structure to read: the dataset repo holds 8 files, all
CSV/README, no structures; the template's `tests/.../structures.zip` is 12 synthetic
one-alanine placeholders. Step 1 in its literal form returned nothing — step 2 answered it.

### 11.4 What the audit *did* find: a PoseBusters gate that zeroes 3 of the 20

Reading the backend turned up a second, **real and larger** hazard, which nothing in this
repo had accounted for. After OST scoring, `score_single_structure` runs
`PoseBusters(config="dock")` on the **predicted** complex — `mol_pred` = the `LIG`
fragment, `mol_cond` = every other fragment combined (so the heme is part of "protein") —
and with `POSEBUSTERS_MAX_FAILURES = 0` (`backend/config.py`) **any single failed check
overrides LDDT-PLI and LDDT-LP to 0.0 and BiSyRMSD to 20 Å.** Coverage stays 1.0, so it
does not even read as a failure.

That gate was replicated verbatim (posebusters 0.6.5, same calls, same
`AssignBondOrdersFromTemplate` from the challenge SMILES) and run on both zips:

| zip | n | **ZEROED** | pass | failing check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `02_drop.zip` | 20 | **3** (`OCNT-2313038`, `OCNT-2313429`, `OCNT-2395342`) | 17 | `minimum_distance_to_protein`, 3/3 |
| `02_blind_rehearsal_val87b.zip` | 87 | **10** | 77 | `minimum_distance_to_protein`, 10/10 |

**It is not the iron.** `OCNT-2312554` coordinates at Fe–N **2.12 Å** and passes; the three
failures are ordinary steric clashes — LIG C44···PHE213 CE1 at **2.46 Å** (2313038),
LIG N35···ARG372 O at **2.27 Å** (2313429), LIG C45···THR309 CG2 at **2.54 Å** (2395342).
`--heme keep` is safe.

**Measured cost on the validation set** (`posebusters_board_impact_val87.json`): the 10
zeroed ligands' *true* LDDT-PLI is 0.5952 mean (0.3521–0.7982), so the board our own
scorer reads as **0.6164 would be scored 0.5480 by the organisers — −0.0684.** On the
drop set, 3 of 20 at the playbook's stratum means is **≈ −0.082**, which moves the §8
pre-announcement from ~0.578 to **~0.50**.

**A rescue exists in the pool already paid for** (`posebusters_pool_rescue_drop.json`,
20 poses × 3 ligands re-run through the same gate):

| ligand | poses passing PoseBusters | shipped pose (xeng rank 1) | first passing pose |
|---|---|---|---|
| OCNT-2313038 | **19 / 20** | fail | xeng rank **2** |
| OCNT-2313429 | **11 / 20** | fail | xeng rank **4** |
| OCNT-2395342 | **13 / 20** | fail | xeng rank **2** |

i.e. `argmin(xeng)` **restricted to PoseBusters-passing poses** costs 1–3 rank positions
and would recover three hard zeros. It is prediction-side (no crystal), so it is not
leaky. **It was NOT applied and no zip was built for it**: it is an unvalidated change to
a frozen selector on drop day, which the playbook forbids in bold, and validating it
properly needs the archived val87b pool. It is the user's call, and it is the one number

### 12.2 A against B, paired over 87 ligands, under the organisers' rule

`scripts/submit/submission_audit.py pbselect` — committed with this section, not throwaway

`PoseBusters(config="dock")`, and 0.0 if it fails**, which is what
`score_single_structure` does at `POSEBUSTERS_MAX_FAILURES = 0`. Per-pose truth comes from
`poses_scored_val87b.csv`; both *rules* are prediction-side, only the *measurement* uses
crystals.

* **A** = `argmin(xeng)`, the shipped selector, unchanged.
* **B** = `argmin(xeng)` restricted to PoseBusters-passing poses, falling back to plain
  `argmin` when a ligand has no passing pose at all.

| board | **gated** (organisers' rule) | ungated (our scorer, no gate) |
|---|---|---|
| **A** — shipped `argmin(xeng)` | **0.5480** | **0.6164** |

### 12.5 Recommendation: submit **03**, not 02

The measurement supports it, and it is the one kind of selector change the playbook's bold
prohibition was never aimed at: **not a retune for score — ungated it is worth −0.0034 —
but the removal of poses the organisers' scorer treats as invalid.** Every input is
prediction-side, so the no-leaky-features rule is satisfied; nothing was fitted, so there
is no fold to hold out.

Sizing it on the drop set, with the caveat that the drop set has no ground truth: 3 of 20
zeroed at the playbook's stratum means is ≈ **−0.082**, which is what pushed the §8
pre-announcement from ~0.578 to ~0.50. Rule B removes all three zeros (20 of 20 now pass)
at a validation-measured true-pose cost of −0.0034 per ligand averaged over the board.
**The §8 pre-announcement therefore reverts to ~0.54–0.58, centred on ~0.575**, and stands
as the number to judge the entry by.

What could still make this the wrong call, stated rather than buried:

1. **n = 10.** The gated delta is carried by ten validation ligands and the Wilcoxon p is
   at its floor for that n. The *direction* is not in doubt (a zero cannot get worse), but
   the magnitude is.
2. **Version dependence.** The gate was replicated with **posebusters 0.6.5** and RDKit's
   `AssignBondOrdersFromTemplate` from the challenge SMILES. A different upstream version,
   or a different `mol_cond` assembly, could zero a different set. We match their code as
   read on 2026-09-24; we cannot match their environment.
3. **Blind transfer.** The drop set is 45% Type I against the validation set's 16% (§4).
   Nothing in the gate is coordination-related, and the failing check is steric, so there
   is no reason to expect the transfer to break — but it is an extrapolation.

If any of those worries dominate, 02 is still a valid, complete submission and both zips
are on disk. **Nothing has been submitted, uploaded, POSTed or pushed.** The choice is the
user's.
